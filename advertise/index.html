Advertise your giveaway on GiveOwl

GiveOwl is a free overview of giveaways, sweepstakes and online advent calendars, on giveowl.com and in the GiveOwl app for Android, in 19 languages. A sponsored placement puts your entry at the top of your country’s list.

Also in: Norsk · Deutsch · Dansk · Svenska

Where we are today

GiveOwl launched in October 2026. We don’t have big traffic numbers to show yet, and we don’t use analytics or tracking tools on our visitors. The only measurement is an anonymous count of clicks on shop links (see our privacy policy), so we can’t promise views or clicks. That is why prices are low while we grow. Add UTM tags to your link to see visits from GiveOwl in your own analytics.

Basic listing: always free

Every genuine giveaway or advent calendar can be listed for free, whether you advertise or not. Paying never decides what we list.

What a sponsored placement includes

  • Top of your country’s list in the app’s Explore tab and on giveowl.com/browse/. There are never more than two sponsored entries in a list at a time.
  • Top of your country’s page on giveowl.com (for example giveowl.com/c/no/). With the advent package, also top of the advent calendar list and page.
  • Your own entry page with the prize, deadline and how to enter, linking straight to your page.
  • A highlighted card with the label “Sponsored”.
  • Your own tracking link (UTM) if you want one.

Always labelled, same rules

  • Sponsored entries always carry the label “Sponsored” (“Annonse” in Norwegian), and links are marked rel="sponsored".
  • The same quality rules as every listing apply: a real organizer, official terms, correct dates and prizes, and free to enter or clearly marked “Purchase required”.
  • We don’t accept lead-generation schemes, gambling, alcohol or promotions aimed at children, and we may decline any booking.
  • A sponsored entry that has ended is removed from the top straight away.
