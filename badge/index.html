Badge & widget

Two free ways to link to GiveOwl. Nothing is required: every genuine giveaway is listed whether you use them or not.

“Featured on GiveOwl” badge for organizers

Is your giveaway or advent calendar listed on GiveOwl? Put the badge on your campaign page so visitors can see it has been checked and can find more giveaways. Pick your entry, and the badge links to your own page on GiveOwl.

For blogs, forums and newsletters with a web version. The widget lists the online advent calendars with a door open today (from 1 December; before that, the calendars that are coming) and updates itself every day.
