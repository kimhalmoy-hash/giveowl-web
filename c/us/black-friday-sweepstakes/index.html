Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy something to enter?

US sweepstakes generally have to offer a free way to enter – that is what "no purchase necessary" in the official rules means. We only list sweepstakes with a free entry method. If buying something gives bonus entries we say so, and anything that needs a purchase is marked.

When do Black Friday sweepstakes run?

Most run in November around Thanksgiving week and end on or shortly after Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday. The exact end date and time zone are in each sponsor's official rules and shown on each entry here.

Who can enter?

Usually legal US residents aged 18 or older, sometimes with state exclusions or a higher minimum age. We note eligibility on each entry, but the official rules are what count.
