Frequently asked questions

Do I need to enter every day?

Usually yes: each day is normally its own drawing with its own prize. The official rules say whether an entry carries over to later days.

Are 12 Days giveaways free?

The ones we list have a free way to enter. Some are run on social media as well as by web form; we only list promotions where you can enter without buying anything.

When do they start?

Dates vary by sponsor – some start before Thanksgiving, many in early December. The start and end dates are on each entry.
