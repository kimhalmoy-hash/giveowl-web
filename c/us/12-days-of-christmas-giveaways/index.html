Frequently asked questions

Do I need to enter every day?

Usually yes: each day is normally its own drawing with its own prize. The official rules say whether an entry carries over to later days.

Are 12 Days giveaways free?

The purchase rule is on each giveaway, according to the sponsor's official rules, not one rule for the whole page. We can't guarantee that none needs a purchase. 4 of the 9 are open now, and 5 are coming. 9 of 9 listed are marked without a purchase in our data. 8 of the 9 editorially confirmed for 2026 have a checked terms note. A missing confirmation flag is not itself a reading of the official rules. A stored field is not a new reading of the official rules, and a 2025 note doesn't count as a 2026 check. Some are run on social media as well as by web form.

When do they start?

Dates vary by sponsor – some start before Thanksgiving, many in early December. The start and end dates are on each entry.
