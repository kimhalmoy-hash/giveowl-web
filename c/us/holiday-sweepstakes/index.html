Frequently asked questions

When do holiday sweepstakes run?

Mostly from early November through December. Black Friday promotions usually close within days of Black Friday; Christmas sweepstakes often run into mid or late December. The end date is on each entry.

Do I have to buy something to enter?

No. US sweepstakes generally have to offer a free way to enter ("no purchase necessary"). Anything that needs a purchase is clearly marked.

Who can enter?

Usually legal US residents aged 18 or older (21 or older for alcohol brands), and some sweepstakes exclude certain states. Check the eligibility on each entry and the official rules.
