Frequently asked questions

Are travel sweepstakes free to enter?

Yes for everything we list, unless it is clearly marked as needing a purchase. US sweepstakes must offer a free way to enter.

Do I have to pay tax on a trip prize?

In the US, prizes are generally taxable income at their approximate retail value (ARV). The official rules state the ARV.

Can I choose my travel dates?

It depends on the sponsor; many trips have blackout dates or fixed dates. Check the official rules.
