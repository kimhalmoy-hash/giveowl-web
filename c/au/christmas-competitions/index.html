Frequently asked questions

Are these competitions free to enter?

Yes, everything listed here is free to enter unless it is clearly marked as needing a purchase.

Who can enter?

Usually Australian residents aged 18 or over; some are limited to certain states or territories. The terms and conditions decide.

Is the winner drawn at random?

It depends. Some Australian competitions are games of chance (a random draw), others are games of skill judged on a short answer. The terms say which, and when winners are drawn or judged.
