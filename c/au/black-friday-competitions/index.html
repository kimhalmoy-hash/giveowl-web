Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday is 30 November. Many competitions start earlier and some close before Black Friday, so the closing date on each entry is what counts.

Do I have to buy something to enter?

No. Everything listed here can be entered without buying anything, and the promoter's terms say how. If a competition needs a purchase, it is marked.

Who can enter?

Usually Australian residents aged 18 or over, though some promoters set a different minimum age. Some competitions may be limited to certain states or territories. The terms and conditions decide.
