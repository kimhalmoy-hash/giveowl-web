Frequently asked questions

Are these contests free to enter?

Everything listed here can be entered without buying anything, and the organizer's rules say how. If a contest needs a purchase, it is marked.

Who can enter?

Usually residents of Canada who are 18 or older, or who have reached the age of majority where they live. Some contests can be limited to certain provinces or territories, or exclude some of them. Eligibility is noted on each entry, and the official rules decide.

Will the prize arrive before Christmas?

It depends on the closing date and on the organizer. Not every contest promises delivery before Christmas, so check the rules.
