Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026. Many contests start earlier in the autumn and some close before Black Friday, so the closing date on each entry is what counts.

Do I have to buy something to enter?

No. Everything listed here can be entered without buying anything, and the organizer's rules say how. If a contest needs a purchase, it is marked.

Who can enter?

Usually residents of Canada who are 18 or older, or who have reached the age of majority where they live. Some contests can be limited to certain provinces or territories, or exclude some of them. Eligibility is noted on each entry, and the official rules decide.
