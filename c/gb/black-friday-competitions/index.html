Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday is 30 November. Many UK retailers run a "Black Week" from Monday 23 November, and some start earlier.

Do I have to buy something to enter?

No. Everything listed here can be entered free. Some draws also give an automatic entry with an order, but the free route has the same chance of winning – the T&Cs say so.

Who can enter?

Usually UK residents aged 18 or over; some include the Channel Islands. The terms and conditions decide.
