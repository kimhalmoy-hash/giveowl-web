When do the open Black Friday competitions close?

Are these the same as Black Friday sale deals?

No. This page lists UK prize draws and competitions. Retail Black Friday sale deals, such as shop discounts, are not listed here.

Do US sites call these sweepstakes?

US sites often say sweepstakes. This UK page lists prize draws and competitions.

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday is 30 November. Many UK retailers run a "Black Week" from Monday 23 November, and some start earlier.
