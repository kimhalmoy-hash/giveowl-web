Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy anything to enter?

2 of the 26 open now are marked as needing a purchase (Wickedly Welsh Chocolate and Mars (Pedigree & Dreamies) at B&M); 24 are marked without a purchase in our data. The terms on each entry decide.

Are UK Christmas competitions free to enter?

Not all of them. The purchase rule is on each competition, according to the organiser's terms, not one rule for the whole page. 24 of the 26 open now are marked without a purchase in our data, and 6 have a checked terms note. A stored field is not a new reading of the terms.

Who can enter?

Most are open to UK residents aged 18 or over; some are limited to England, Scotland and Wales or to a local area. The terms and conditions decide.

Will the prize arrive before Christmas?

It depends on the closing date. Many Christmas draws close in early or mid-December, but not all promise delivery before Christmas – check the T&Cs.

When do most UK Christmas competitions close?

24 of 26 with a closing date close on or before 24 December. The earliest closes on 11 November, the latest on 30 January. Anything past its closing date is removed.

What is a 12 Days of Christmas competition?

A competition that runs over 12 days, usually in December, with a new prize each day. Often each day is a separate prize draw, so you can enter again the next day – the organiser's terms say whether that applies. The confirmed ones are in the 12 Days of Christmas section on this page.
