Frequently asked questions

Are UK Christmas competitions free to enter?

Prize draws in the UK generally need a free way to enter. Everything here is free unless it is clearly marked as needing a purchase.

Who can enter?

Most are open to UK residents aged 18 or over; some are limited to England, Scotland and Wales or to a local area. The terms and conditions decide.

Will the prize arrive before Christmas?

It depends on the closing date. Many Christmas draws close in early or mid-December, but not all promise delivery before Christmas – check the T&Cs.
