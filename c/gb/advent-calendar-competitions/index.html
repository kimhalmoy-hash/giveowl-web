Frequently asked questions

When do advent competitions start?

Most online advent calendars open on 1 December and run to 24 or 25 December; some start in late November. Start dates are shown on each entry once the organiser has published them.

Are advent calendar competitions free to enter?

Yes – everything listed here is free to enter. Some need you to register or use an app; it says so on each entry.

What does "expected" mean?

The organiser ran an advent calendar in 2025 at the same address but hasn't confirmed 2026 yet. We mark it confirmed as soon as this year's calendar is live.

Do I need to enter every day?

Usually each door is its own prize draw, so entering every day gives you more chances. Some calendars put all entries into one final draw – the T&Cs say which.
