    Entering safely

    • Canadian contests often exclude Quebec or require a skill-testing question before a prize is awarded – the official rules say which, plus the age of majority in your province.
    • Never pay a fee or give card details to claim a prize, and enter only on the promoter's own site or app.
    • Anything that needs a purchase is marked in the list.

    FAQ

    How many contests are in this list?

    28 contests: 7 Christmas-themed (some close after Christmas) and 21 other open contests closing by December 25. Each is checked on the organizer's own page and the list updates itself.

    When do they close?

    Each one has its own closing date, shown in the list. The first closes on November 11, 2026 (iCash (Finabanx Ltd.)) and the last on January 2, 2027 (Coca-Cola Canada (Fuze Iced Tea)).

    Do I have to buy anything?

    No: according to the organizers, none of the 28 contests listed needs a purchase. The official rules apply.

    Can Quebec residents enter, and what is a skill-testing question?

    9 of the 28 contests exclude Quebec according to the rules, so Quebec residents should check each one. 16 state that a selected winner must correctly answer a skill-testing question before the prize is awarded. The official rules on the organizer's page decide.
