Entering safely

  • Canadian contests often exclude Quebec or require a skill-testing question before a prize is awarded – the official rules say which, plus the age of majority in your province.
  • Never pay a fee or give card details to claim a prize, and enter only on the promoter's own site or app.
  • Anything that needs a purchase is marked in the list.
