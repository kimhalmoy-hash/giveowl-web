    Entering safely

    • Most Australian competitions are open to residents aged 18+, and some are limited to certain states or territories. Some are games of skill judged on a short answer rather than a random draw – the terms say which.
    • Never pay a fee or give card details to claim a prize, and enter only on the promoter's own site or app.
    • Anything that needs a purchase is marked in the list.

    FAQ

    How many competitions are in this list?

    31 competitions: 14 Christmas-themed (some close after Christmas) and 17 other open competitions closing by 25 December. Each is checked on the promoter's own page and the list updates itself.

    When do they close?

    Each one has its own closing date, shown in the list. The first closes on 15 November 2026 (Bella Boheme) and the last on 4 January 2027 (HarperCollins Publishers Australia).

    Do I have to buy anything?

    No: according to the promoters, none of the 31 competitions listed needs a purchase. The terms and conditions apply.
