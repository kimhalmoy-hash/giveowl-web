Christmas in the UK: Traditions and Recipes 2026

Published October 24, 2026 · GiveOwl

Stir-up Sunday, mince pies, crackers and Boxing Day: many British Christmas customs have a long history, and the stories behind them are often more tangled than they look. Here are the key dates for 2026, short histories with sources, and simple recipes you can make yourself.

Key dates 2026

  • Stir-up Sunday (last Sunday before Advent): Sunday 22 November
  • Advent Sunday (the first Sunday of Advent): Sunday 29 November
  • First advent calendar door: Tuesday 1 December
  • Christmas Eve: Thursday 24 December
  • Christmas Day: Friday 25 December (bank holiday)
  • Boxing Day: Saturday 26 December; substitute bank holiday Monday 28 December
  • Twelfth Night: 5 January

Stir-up Sunday and Advent Sunday are our own calculation from the calendar. The bank holiday dates come from GOV.UK.

Boxing Day

Boxing Day (26 December) falls on a Saturday in 2026, so the substitute bank holiday is Monday 28 December, according to GOV.UK. Christmas Day is Friday 25 December. The name most likely comes from "Christmas boxes", the money or gifts that were given to servants, apprentices and tradespeople. Read more in Christmas dinner and Boxing Day traditions.
