How UK prize draws work

  • Most are free prize draws: winners are picked at random after the closing date. Read the terms for the closing date and time, who can enter (often UK residents aged 18+) and how winners are contacted.
  • Some are entered by text message or need a purchase – those are marked in the list, and the terms say what it costs.
  • Never pay a fee or give card details to claim a prize, and enter only on the organiser's own site or app.
