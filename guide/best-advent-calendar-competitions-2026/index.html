Tips for advent calendar season

  • Usually each door is its own prize draw, so entering every day gives you more chances. Some calendars put all entries into one final draw – the T&Cs say which.
  • Only enter on the organiser's own website or app. Genuine promoters never ask you to pay or give card details to receive a prize, and a promotion on Facebook isn't allowed to require you to share it or tag friends.
  • Use a separate email address for competitions and check your spam folder in December: winners often have to reply within a set time.
