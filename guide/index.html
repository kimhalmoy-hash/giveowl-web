How we pick what goes into a guide

  • Every listed giveaway or advent calendar links to the organiser's own page. We never copy lists from aggregator sites.
  • Prizes, dates and rules come from the organiser. If something is not stated there, the guide says so instead of guessing.
  • You always enter with the organiser, not on GiveOwl.

Before you enter any giveaway

  • Check who runs it: a real company name and official rules or terms should be easy to find.
  • You should never have to pay, or give card details, to claim a prize.
  • Be careful with messages saying you have won something you did not enter, and with look-alike social media accounts.
  • Read who can enter (country, age) and the closing date before you spend time on it.
