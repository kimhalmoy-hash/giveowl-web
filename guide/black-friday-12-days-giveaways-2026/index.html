Entering safely

  • No purchase necessary. US sweepstakes generally have to offer a free way to enter. We only list sweepstakes with a free entry method; anything that needs a purchase is marked.
  • Check eligibility. Most are for legal US residents 18 or older, and some exclude certain states. The official rules decide.
  • Never pay to claim a prize. A genuine sponsor won't ask for a fee or your card details to send you a prize. Enter only on the sponsor's own site or app.
  • Watch your inbox. Winners are often notified by email and may have to respond by a deadline, so check your spam folder.
