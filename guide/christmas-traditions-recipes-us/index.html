Christmas in the US: Traditions and Recipes 2026

Published October 24, 2026 · GiveOwl

American Christmas is a mix of English, Dutch and German customs that grew up on this side of the Atlantic, and it starts with Thanksgiving. Here are the key dates for 2026, short histories with sources, and simple recipes you can make yourself.

MessengerPinterestFacebookWhatsAppX✉

Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24, 2026; Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026.

Key dates 2026

  • Thanksgiving: Thursday, November 26 (federal holiday)
  • Black Friday: Friday, November 27
  • Advent Sunday (first Sunday of Advent): Sunday, November 29
  • Cyber Monday: Monday, November 30
  • First advent calendar door: Tuesday, December 1
  • Hanukkah: begins at sundown on Friday, December 4, 2026 and ends at nightfall on Saturday, December 12, 2026
  • Christmas Eve: Thursday, December 24
  • Christmas Day: Friday, December 25 (federal holiday)
  • Kwanzaa: December 26 to January 1

Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November, so in 2026 there are 29 days from Thanksgiving to Christmas Day (our calculation). Advent Sunday is also our calculation. Federal holiday dates come from OPM, Black Friday and Cyber Monday from timeanddate.com, Hanukkah from Hebcal and Kwanzaa from the Smithsonian's National Museum of African American History and Culture.
