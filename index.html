What GiveOwl does

One clean list instead of hundreds of tabs.

Made for your country

GiveOwl picks your country from your device. US first, plus the UK, Canada, Norway, Sweden and more. Change it any time.

No purchase necessary

The filter is on by default, so you only see giveaways you can enter for free. Deadlines, prizes and how to enter at a glance.

In November and December, online advent calendars get their own tab, with today’s doors from your favorites.
